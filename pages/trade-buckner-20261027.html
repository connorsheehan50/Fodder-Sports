49ers Bring Back DeForest Buckner

San Francisco reacquired the defensive tackle it traded to Indianapolis in 2020, reuniting with a former cornerstone of Kyle Shanahan's defense.

The Summary

  • The terms: San Francisco gets DeForest Buckner. Indianapolis gets a 2027 third-round pick and a 2028 fourth-round pick.
  • Buckner, 32, originally played six seasons in San Francisco before the Colts traded for him in 2020. He made four Pro Bowls with Indianapolis.
  • Why Indianapolis did it: the Colts were 2-5 and moving veterans. Buckner was in the final year of his deal.
  • Why San Francisco did it: the 49ers needed interior line depth after injuries on the defensive front, and saw an opportunity to add a familiar face before the deadline.

What the San Francisco Media Is Saying About the 49ers

  • Nostalgic and practical. The Bay Area coverage leads with the homecoming angle — Buckner was a fan favorite — and pivots to the football case: an experienced interior rusher who knows this scheme.

What the Indianapolis Media Is Saying About the Colts

  • Accepting. Colts coverage had seen this coming after the season turned. Most pieces focus on what Indianapolis gets back and whether the picks land in useful spots.
