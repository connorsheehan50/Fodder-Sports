Seahawks Get Quenton Nelson to Fix Their Line

Indianapolis moved the perennial All-Pro guard to Seattle, where he immediately became the anchor the defending champions needed.

The Summary

  • The terms: Seattle gets Quenton Nelson and a 2028 fourth-round pick. Indianapolis gets a 2027 second-round pick and a 2028 third-round pick.
  • Nelson, 29, is a six-time Pro Bowler and four-time First Team All-Pro. He was entering the final year of his contract with the Colts.
  • Why Indianapolis did it: the Colts were 2-5 and in rebuilding mode heading into the stretch run.
  • Why Seattle did it: the defending champions needed interior offensive line help and moved before the Nov. 10 deadline to secure it.
  • Nelson debuted Nov. 2 in Seattle's home game against the Bears.

What the Seattle Media Is Saying About the Seahawks

  • Enthusiastic. Seattle coverage calls the trade a classic championship-window move: the Seahawks gave up future picks for a proven starter who could anchor the line through a Super Bowl run.
  • Nelson reunites with the type of dominant offensive line philosophy the Seahawks have been building around Geno Smith.

What the Indianapolis Media Is Saying About the Colts

  • Resigned but rational. Colts coverage acknowledges the team's direction changed once the season slipped away, and frames the return as a reasonable price for a 29-year-old with one year left on his deal.
