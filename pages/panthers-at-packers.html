Carolina still leads the NFC South despite a rough Sunday. Green Bay was one red-zone finish away from beating Detroit. Thursday night, one of them gets back on track.

The summary

  • Both teams lost on Sunday. Green Bay fell 20–17 at Detroit after scoring seven points on four red-zone trips. Carolina lost 43–13 to Tampa Bay but still sits first in the NFC South at 3–3.
  • This is a meeting of two of the league's best passing offenses: Carolina ranks second in passing yards per game and Green Bay fifth.
  • The run game is the swing matchup. Green Bay has the league's lowest-ranked rushing offense, and Carolina allows the most rushing yards per game (153.2). Packers running back Josh Jacobs returned in Week 7.
  • Green Bay's defense leads the NFL with nine interceptions and 13 takeaways.
  • Green Bay is a 3.5-point favorite, and the total has climbed from 47.5 to 48.5 since it opened.

What the Carolina media is saying about the Panthers

  • Time to bounce back fast. Panthers quarterback Bryce Young owned the Tampa Bay loss, saying the turnovers were "always my fault," and coach Dave Canales says Young is "ready to rock" for Thursday.
  • The plan is quick throws. Young faces pressure on 44.3% of dropbacks against four or fewer rushers, the highest rate among qualified passers, so Carolina wants to get the ball out early.
  • The line is patched together. Right tackle Taylor Moton is out for the season, and rookie Monroe Freeling starts in his place. "It's one thing getting beat, but it's another thing beating yourself," Freeling said.

What the Green Bay media is saying about the Panthers

  • Don't judge Carolina by one Sunday. Green Bay coverage points to wins over Detroit and Atlanta, and to Young's 12 touchdown passes against four interceptions.
  • Panthers receiver Tetairoa McMillan, last season's Offensive Rookie of the Year (511 yards in six games), and running back Chuba Hubbard (two 100-yard games) are the players to watch.
  • The new-look defense is getting attention too, led by linebacker Devin Lloyd, who has two interceptions.

What the Green Bay media is saying about the Packers

  • The red zone, not the officiating, cost them in Detroit. Green Bay coverage counts three wasted chances inside the 20. "You gotta finish plays," Packers coach Matt LaFleur said.
  • The defense gets credit for a second straight strong week against a high-scoring offense.
  • Packers quarterback Jordan Love called for a better run game, and Carolina's run defense is the chance to find one. This is the last of three home games in 19 days before a trip to New England.

What the Carolina media is saying about the Packers

  • Respect. Panthers safety Nick Scott called Green Bay "extremely talented, better in real life than they are on paper."
  • Carolina coverage expects a pass-heavy night: Green Bay throws on 66.9% of plays, the highest rate in the league, and is also the league's most penalized team.

What everyone else is saying about this game

  • Panthers: Tampa Bay ran for 258 yards on Sunday, and national previews expect Green Bay to test that run defense right away. Carolina needs Young to get the ball out fast and McMillan and defensive tackle Derrick Brown to win their matchups.
  • Packers: National coverage sees Love and pass rusher Lukas Van Ness as the matchup edge. Van Ness has 3.5 sacks, and Green Bay ranks fourth in pressure rate without blitzing.
  • Packers: Every national pick we found takes Green Bay, mostly by a touchdown or less.

Who's out

Status from the final injury reports on Wednesday, Oct. 28.

  • Out: Panthers pass rusher Jaelan Phillips (hamstring).
  • Out: Panthers right tackle Taylor Moton, placed on injured reserve after Week 7.
  • Out: Panthers cornerback Akayleb Evans (triceps, knee). Cornerback Jaycee Horn is also on injured reserve, leaving Carolina thin at the position.
  • Out: Panthers guard Damien Lewis.
  • In: Panthers quarterback Bryce Young (knee) and receiver Jalen Coker (shoulder) practiced fully and carry no designation.
  • Out: Packers pass rusher Micah Parsons remains on the PUP list. He could return to practice next week.
  • Out: Packers linebacker Zaire Franklin (shin).
  • Out: Packers center Sean Rhyan (high-ankle sprain).
  • Uncertain: Packers second-year receiver Matthew Golden (shoulder, foot) is questionable after a limited practice Wednesday. "I sure hope so," Packers coach Matt LaFleur said when asked if Golden will play.
  • Uncertain: Packers defensive tackle Devonte Wyatt is questionable.

Team connections

  • Rasheed Walker, a former Packers draft pick, starts at left tackle for Carolina against his old team.
  • AJ Dillon, a former Packers running back, is Carolina's backup running back.
  • Darrell Bevell, Carolina's associate head coach, spent six seasons with the Packers.
  • Carolina won the last meeting 16–13 at Lambeau Field in November 2025, on a game-winning field goal by Panthers kicker Ryan Fitzgerald.
