Cardinals Trade Marvin Harrison Jr. to Houston

Arizona sent the second-year receiver to the Texans in a move that rattled the fan base and reshuffled the AFC South.

The Summary

  • The terms: Houston gets Marvin Harrison Jr. and a 2027 sixth-round pick. Arizona gets a 2027 second-round pick and a 2027 seventh-round pick. Early reports had the late-round picks reversed; the Cardinals' official announcement corrected the terms.
  • Harrison was the fourth overall pick in the 2025 NFL Draft and appeared in all 16 games as a rookie before the trade.
  • Why Arizona did it: the Cardinals entered the week at 1-4 and had reportedly received permission from Harrison's camp to explore trades.
  • Why Houston did it: the Texans addressed their receiver depth with a potential long-term piece alongside Nico Collins.

What the Houston Media Is Saying About the Texans

  • Excited. Houston coverage frames the trade as a franchise-defining move, adding one of the league's highest-upside receivers while still in his rookie contract.
  • The price looks reasonable. Harrison's value was at a low point after a slow start to 2026, so the Texans got him cheaper than expected.

What the Arizona Media Is Saying About the Cardinals

  • Mixed. Some Arizona coverage calls it a good return for a player the team was rebuilding around, while fans are more critical of trading a top-five pick after just one-plus seasons.
