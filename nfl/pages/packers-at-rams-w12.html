The summary

  • The #12-ranked Packers (6-4) and #11-ranked Rams (6-4) meet in the NFL's first Thanksgiving Eve game at SoFi Stadium, with both teams chasing NFC wild-card spots behind their division leaders.
  • Packers edge rusher Micah Parsons was medically cleared from his knee injury and makes his season debut. Running back Josh Jacobs and linebacker Zaire Franklin are inactive.
  • The Rams have won four straight, and their defense has allowed three touchdowns in its last five games, the fewest in the league. Running back Blake Corum has scored in six straight games.
  • Los Angeles lost receiver Puka Nacua, likely for the season, after knee surgery on a completely torn MCL, and receiver Davante Adams is also out. Sean McVay has turned to a new two-back package to keep the offense moving.
  • Jordan Love ranks second in the league in passing yards, with receivers Christian Watson and Matthew Golden both in the top 10 in receiving yards. McVay is 0-5 against Matt LaFleur.

The word on the Packers

  • Green Bay media is all in on Parsons. Coverage says his return could energize the whole roster, and safety Javon Bullard praised his work ethic and leadership.
  • Local analysts feel the urgency. Team coverage calls this game too important to downplay, with both teams 6-4 and looking up at division leaders, and the Packers coming off a 30-10 home loss to Minnesota.
  • Los Angeles coverage of the Packers expects a defensive battle. Rams previews see two talented fronts and say Matthew Stafford has to beat Green Bay's blitz.
  • Los Angeles media is watching Parsons too. Local coverage frames the night around Parsons' debut against the Rams' own healthy pass rush.

The word on the Rams

  • Los Angeles media is worried about the receivers. Coverage focuses on Nacua's torn MCL and asks whether the Rams should have added a receiver at the deadline or brought back Brandin Cooks.
  • Local analysts call it a must-win. Rams coverage says Los Angeles has to beat Green Bay to control its own playoff fate, and points to a 2025 draft class that has produced little.
  • Green Bay's coverage of the Rams sees a hot team. Packers previews note the teams have matching records but are heading in opposite directions, with the Rams on a four-game streak and the league's stingiest defense lately.
  • Green Bay media is studying McVay's adjustments. Local writers break down the Rams' new two-running-back package built to work around the receiver injuries.

What the national media is saying

  • Packers: National coverage of Green Bay centers on Parsons' return and how much it can lift the defense.
  • Packers: National critics question the Packers' offensive line, and some doubt Green Bay holds its playoff spot.
  • Rams: National writers see Corum reshaping the Rams' backfield and the run game carrying the offense.
  • The game: National previews call it close to a must-win for both teams in a crowded NFC wild-card race.

Who's out

  • Out: Packers running back Josh Jacobs (finger) and linebacker Zaire Franklin (shin), both inactive.
  • Questionable: Packers guards Travis Glover (shoulder) and Kevin Zeitler (triceps), center Sean Rhyan (ankle), and receivers Bo Melton (thigh) and Skyy Moore (ankle).
  • In: Packers edge rusher Micah Parsons (knee) was medically cleared and is set to make his season debut.
  • Out: Rams guard Steve Avila (hamstring), tight end Terrance Ferguson (ankle, hamstring), and center Coleman Shelton (hamstring).
  • Out for season: Rams receiver Puka Nacua (knee) is likely out for the season after surgery.
