The summary

  • The #5-ranked Broncos (8-2) beat Las Vegas 38-24 behind rookie running back Jonah Coleman's 111 yards and three touchdowns, which earned him AFC Offensive Player of the Week. Denver is chasing the AFC West title and the conference's top seed.
  • The #15-ranked Steelers (6-4) have lost two straight and hold the AFC's last wild-card spot. Mason Rudolph starts at quarterback after the team benched and then released Aaron Rodgers this week.
  • Rodgers accused head coach Mike McCarthy of sleeping in meetings in a public interview Wednesday. McCarthy said the benching was his call, and the organization has backed him, according to reports.
  • Denver is without running back J.K. Dobbins, whom head coach Sean Payton suspended for conduct detrimental to the team, along with defensive end Sai'vion Jones and safety Taylor Rapp.
  • Kickoff is 3 p.m. ET Friday in Pittsburgh.

The word on the Broncos

  • Denver media is all in on Coleman. Local coverage calls the rookie's rise a phenomenon and says Dobbins' suspension should not take away from it.
  • Local analysts are focused on the run game. Broncos previews say running the ball and stopping the run are the keys, and that the Rodgers drama made Friday more interesting.
  • Pittsburgh's coverage of the Broncos calls them a good team. Steelers previews see an 8-2 Denver team as a hard opponent to regroup against.
  • Pittsburgh media is studying Denver's offense and defense. Local previews break down what to expect from both sides of the ball after a week dominated by the quarterback story.

The word on the Steelers

  • Pittsburgh media is in turmoil. Coverage describes an ugly stretch of football and major questions about the McCarthy tenure after Rodgers' exit.
  • Local analysts want the focus back on football. Previews say the team still has a game to play and a chance to regroup at home.
  • Denver's coverage of the Steelers sees a chance. Broncos previews note Pittsburgh is in a key AFC race and scout a team that just lost its starting quarterback.
  • Denver media sees the drama as a bonus. Local writers had been looking forward to sacking Rodgers and now plan for Rudolph instead.

What the national media is saying

  • Steelers: National coverage of the Steelers is harsh, with pieces calling the organization asleep at the wheel and dysfunctional after the Rodgers saga.
  • Steelers: League executives and scouts quoted in national coverage believe Rodgers' career is likely over.
  • Broncos: National coverage sees Denver with a real shot at a second straight AFC West title and the conference's top seed.
  • Broncos: Coleman's AFC Offensive Player of the Week award drew national attention.

Who's out

  • Out: Broncos defensive end Sai'vion Jones (hamstring) and safety Taylor Rapp (quadriceps).
  • Out: Broncos running back J.K. Dobbins (suspended by the team).
  • In: No Steelers player received a game status on the final injury report. Aaron Rodgers has been released.
