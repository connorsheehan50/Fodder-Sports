The summary

  • The #21-ranked Patriots (5-5) visit the #32-ranked Chargers (1-9), the team they beat 16-3 in last season's wild-card round. New England, last season's AFC champion, comes off its bye on a two-game losing streak, a game behind Cleveland and Pittsburgh for a wild-card spot.
  • Patriots quarterback Drake Maye is out at least six weeks with a broken fibula, so Tommy DeVito, who has eight career starts, makes his first start for New England. Receiver A.J. Brown is also out with a hamstring injury.
  • Chargers quarterback Justin Herbert is in the concussion protocol after he was sacked six times in a 27-24 loss to the Jets. The Chargers are winless at home.
  • DraftKings has the Patriots favored by 1.5, with a total of 35.5, the lowest of the week.

The word on the Patriots

  • New England media is frustrated with the defense. Local coverage says the defense, not the quarterback situation, is the biggest obstacle to the playoffs, after reports that opponents call it soft.
  • The Brown trade is a sore spot. Local writers call A.J. Brown's first season in New England a nightmare and ask whether the deal belongs among the worst trades in Boston sports history.
  • Los Angeles coverage sees a break for the Chargers' defense. Chargers writers say Maye's injury gives the Bolts' defense a break, with DeVito leading an offense also missing Brown.
  • Los Angeles has said little else about New England. Chargers coverage this week has focused on its own season, the draft, and Harbaugh.

The word on the Chargers

  • Los Angeles media is looking to next year. Local coverage says the season was lost before 1-9 and that the rest of the year is about sorting out the roster, and some say it is time for a hard conversation about Harbaugh.
  • Herbert's health comes first. Local writers would not be surprised if the Chargers sit Herbert rather than rush him back for a game that means little.
  • New England's coverage of the Chargers sees a team that gets in its own way. Patriots writers point to the league's third-highest turnover rate and second-worst scoring rate.
  • New England respects the Chargers' defense. Patriots coverage says the Chargers' defense has been solid, eighth-best at keeping opponents from scoring, despite no help from the offense.

What the national media is saying

  • Patriots: National coverage says everything that could go wrong has gone wrong for Mike Vrabel's Patriots, from injuries to off-field problems.
  • Patriots: National writers report opponents calling New England's defense soft, and some urge the Patriots to sign a veteran quarterback.
  • Chargers: National coverage blasts Harbaugh after the Jets loss, the only home loss of the week.
  • Chargers: National writers say Herbert's disaster season is hiding other major problems.

Who's out

  • Did not practice: Patriots quarterback Drake Maye (out, broken fibula), receiver A.J. Brown, cornerback Carlton Davis III, and tackle Walter Rouse.
  • Limited: Patriots receiver Romeo Doubs, fullback Reggie Gilliam, and guard Alijah Vera-Tucker.
  • Did not practice: Chargers quarterback Justin Herbert (concussion protocol), defensive ends Khalil Mack, Akheem Mesidor, and Tuli Tuipulotu, defensive tackles Teair Tart and Dalvin Tomlinson, linebacker Daiyan Henley, cornerbacks Deane Leonard and Tarheeb Still, safety Kendall Williamson, tight end David Njoku, running back Keaton Mitchell, and receiver Brenen Thompson.
