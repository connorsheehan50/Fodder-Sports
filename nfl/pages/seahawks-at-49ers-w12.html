The summary

  • The #1-ranked 49ers (9-1) host the #10-ranked Seahawks (6-4), who won last season's Super Bowl but would miss the playoffs if the season ended today. San Francisco won the first meeting 32-29 in Seattle.
  • 49ers quarterback Brock Purdy, named NFC Offensive Player of the Week after the 35-10 win over Minnesota, did not practice Wednesday with an ankle injury. "It's not a high ankle, so we'll see," head coach Kyle Shanahan said. "It's day-to-day."
  • Seattle plans to start Sam Darnold, back from injury, over Drew Lock if he makes it through the week. Darnold has nine interceptions in seven games, and Lock has seven touchdown passes and none.
  • San Francisco also held out receiver Mike Evans, who is managing Achilles tendinitis, and lost right tackle Colton McKivitz for the season.

The word on the Seahawks

  • Seattle media is torn on the quarterback. Local coverage says the Seahawks need Darnold because this is not the 2025 team, even as part of the fan base prefers the turnover-free Lock.
  • Local writers call it separation week. Seahawks coverage says the game matters most for any chance at the NFC West, and that the defense, still elite by the numbers, has fallen off from last season.
  • San Francisco's coverage of the Seahawks expects a cleaner game. 49ers writers call the first meeting a strange, turnover-filled game and say the Seahawks' offense likely doesn't score much without those turnovers.
  • San Francisco is on to Seattle. 49ers coverage turned quickly from Mexico City to the Seahawks, with most of the focus on Purdy's ankle.

The word on the 49ers

  • San Francisco media believes this is the year. Local coverage calls the 49ers unquestionably the best team, with Purdy now a legitimate MVP candidate and Shanahan earning his 100th career win.
  • Purdy's ankle is the worry. Tight end George Kittle said he didn't even know about it: "He's a competitor. He's going to play through things that none of us know about."
  • Seattle's coverage of the 49ers sees a Super Bowl favorite. Seahawks writers say this 49ers team feels eerily familiar and looks like a real Super Bowl contender.
  • Seattle is game-planning for Evans. Seahawks coverage asks how to slow Evans, who torched Seattle in the first meeting.

What the national media is saying

  • Seahawks: National coverage is focused on whether Darnold starts and what he gives Seattle against the blitz.
  • Seahawks: National writers see a boost for Seattle with Josh Jones starting on the offensive line.
  • 49ers: National coverage calls Purdy's game against Minnesota a masterclass against the blitz.
  • 49ers: National writers say this will finally be the 49ers' year.

Who's out

  • Did not practice: Seahawks linebacker DeMarcus Lawrence, tackle Abraham Lucas, guard Quenton Nelson, defensive tackle Jarran Reed, and quarterback Jalen Milroe.
  • Limited: Seahawks receiver Jaxon Smith-Njigba, tackle Charles Cross, guard Anthony Bradford, linebacker Ernest Jones, defensive tackle Leonard Williams, tight end Nick Kallerup, and quarterback Drew Lock. Quarterback Sam Darnold was a full participant.
  • Did not practice: 49ers quarterback Brock Purdy, running back Christian McCaffrey, receivers Mike Evans and Jordan Watkins, tackle Trent Williams, and safety Siran Neal.
  • Limited: 49ers receiver Keenan Allen, linebacker Dre Greenlaw, cornerbacks Deommodore Lenoir and Upton Stout, tackle Austen Pleasants, and guard Carver Willis.
