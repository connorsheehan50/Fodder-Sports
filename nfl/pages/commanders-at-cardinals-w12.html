The summary

  • The #19-ranked Commanders (4-5-1) visit the #29-ranked Cardinals (3-8) after beating Cincinnati 22-20 on Monday night, on a field goal as time expired. Washington has won three of its last four and is back in the playoff race.
  • Arizona has lost three straight and 11 straight at home, the longest home losing streak in franchise history. It fired defensive coordinator Nick Rallis after a 34-7 loss in Kansas City and promoted Teryl Austin.
  • Jayden Daniels has thrown seven touchdowns and two interceptions over his last five games, and Arizona has allowed at least 30 points in eight of 11 games. The Cardinals will start either rookie Carson Beck, who has back spasms, or 40-year-old Josh Johnson.
  • Washington signed receiver Brandon Aiyuk this week. DraftKings has the Commanders favored by 6.5.

The word on the Commanders

  • Washington media is riding the momentum. Local coverage celebrates back-to-back wins for the first time this season, with rookie receiver Antonio Williams' two late catches and rookie kicker Drew Stevens' five field goals.
  • Local writers see a defense that is fun to watch. Commanders coverage says this team plays up to the best teams and down to the worst, and that the defense made a statement against Cincinnati.
  • Arizona coverage of the Commanders respects Daniels. "He's a stud. He's a good football player," Cardinals head coach Mike LaFleur said. "I think he's shown that this year, particularly over the last few weeks."
  • Arizona expects a tough test for a new defense. Cardinals coverage says Austin will put his own spin on the defense but cannot change it wholesale with six weeks left.

The word on the Cardinals

  • Arizona media is in evaluation mode. Local coverage calls it a disaster of a season, and writers say general manager Monti Ossenfort should be the next one out the door after Rallis.
  • The quarterback picture is murky. Local writers say Beck's recurring back spasms make it harder to judge whether he can be the long-term starter.
  • Washington's coverage of the Cardinals expects a win. Commanders writers note Washington is favored whoever plays quarterback for Arizona.
  • Washington sees a defense to attack. Commanders previews point out that Arizona has allowed a league-high 25 passing touchdowns.

What the national media is saying

  • Commanders: National coverage credits Daniels' clutch drive against Cincinnati as a reminder of his franchise quarterback potential.
  • Commanders: National writers see the Commanders on the rise in power rankings, led by rookie Antonio Williams.
  • Cardinals: National coverage of Arizona centers on the firing of Rallis after another embarrassing loss.
  • Cardinals: Some national writers say the Cardinals' future is brighter than it looks.
