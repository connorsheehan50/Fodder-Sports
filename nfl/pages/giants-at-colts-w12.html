The summary

  • The #13-ranked Giants (6-3-1), who lead the NFC East, visit the #28-ranked Colts (3-8). New York beat Jacksonville 23-18 as an underdog last week, with two interceptions and 11 quarterback hits on Trevor Lawrence.
  • Indianapolis has lost six of its last seven. After a 26-3 loss in Houston, it released quarterback Daniel Jones and named Anthony Richardson Sr. the starter. It is his first start since Dec. 22, 2024.
  • Giants outside linebacker Abdul Carter was named NFC Defensive Player of the Week after two sacks and five quarterback hits against Jacksonville, though he did not practice Wednesday.
  • This is the last game before New York's schedule turns hard; every other opponent from Week 11 through Week 16 has a winning record.

The word on the Giants

  • New York media believes. Local coverage says the win over Jacksonville shows the Giants really are a good team, and head coach John Harbaugh said, "I thought we played disciplined football, and we played really hard."
  • Local writers warn of a trap. Giants coverage calls this a must-win against a struggling team, before a run of opponents with winning records.
  • Indianapolis coverage of the Giants sees a high bar. Colts writers say the teams that beat New York this season did it by scoring a lot of points, which has not been Indianapolis' strength.
  • Indianapolis respects the NFC East leaders. Colts coverage frames an upset as Richardson needing big plays downfield, the way the Colts won with him in 2024.

The word on the Colts

  • Indianapolis media is fed up. Local coverage calls the Texans loss a national embarrassment and questions whether Steichen keeps his job after the release of Jones and the firing of general manager Chris Ballard.
  • Richardson gets one last chance. "I think we're in a really good place," Steichen said of his relationship with Richardson. "He's matured over these years." Fan confidence rebounded this week, though local writers still have doubts.
  • New York's coverage of the Colts sees a struggling team. Giants writers describe Indianapolis as a 3-8 team that has lost six of seven, and New York is favored on the road.
  • New York knows the danger. Giants coverage says the Colts' change at quarterback is meant to spark an offense that has struggled all season.

What the national media is saying

  • Giants: National coverage asks whether it is time to take the Giants seriously, and some see them as a tough out in the playoffs.
  • Giants: National writers credit a defense that held Jacksonville to its lowest success rate of the season, led by Carter.
  • Colts: National coverage of the Colts is critical, with new hope at quarterback but growing concerns across the offense.
  • Colts: National writers say Colts fans have already moved past Richardson to Steichen's future.

Who's out

  • Did not practice: Giants outside linebacker Abdul Carter, defensive tackle Shelby Harris, and cornerback Andru Phillips.
  • Limited: Giants tight end Isaiah Likely and linebacker Arvell Reese.
  • Did not practice: Colts receiver Deion Burks, tight ends Will Mallory and Andrew Ogletree, running back Seth McGowan, defensive tackle Jerry Tillery, tackle Jalen Travis, and cornerback Charvarius Ward.
  • Out for the season: Colts rookie defensive end Caden Curry.
