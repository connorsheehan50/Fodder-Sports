The summary

  • The #9-ranked Vikings (7-3) host the #24-ranked Falcons (3-7) a week after a 35-10 loss to San Francisco in Mexico City, the worst game of the season for a defense that had been the league's best.
  • Kirk Cousins makes his first home start in his second stint with Minnesota, against the team that signed him in 2024 and then drafted Michael Penix Jr. eighth overall. Cousins lifted the offense right away in a 30-10 win at Green Bay.
  • Atlanta comes off its bye with three straight losses, and Penix has thrown six interceptions in his last three games. The Falcons are still the only team to beat the 49ers this season.
  • Vikings receiver Jordan Addison did not practice Wednesday. DraftKings has Minnesota favored by 7.

The word on the Falcons

  • Atlanta media has turned to next year. Local coverage says the Falcons are basically punting on 2026 at 3-7, tied for last in the NFC South, with talk shifting to free agency and the draft.
  • Penix is the lightning rod. Local writers note his six interceptions in three games and the growing calls for a change, but they do not expect the new staff to bench him.
  • Minnesota's coverage of the Falcons sees a dangerous, inconsistent team. Vikings writers point out that Atlanta is the only team to beat the 49ers and dominated Green Bay at Lambeau, with Bijan Robinson and Drake London able to break open a game.
  • Minnesota remembers last year. Vikings coverage recalls the Falcons' 22-6 win at U.S. Bank Stadium last season, and Robinson called Cousins "my dude" who "brings joy to every team that he goes to."

The word on the Vikings

  • Minnesota media wants a bounce-back. Local coverage calls Atlanta a stress test after the 49ers beat Brian Flores' pressure with quick throws over the middle, with Brock Purdy completing 10 of his first 11 passes for three touchdowns.
  • The Cousins homecoming is the feel-good story. "I think it's a pretty agreed-upon fact among players that U.S. Bank is really the number one place in the league," Cousins said.
  • Atlanta's coverage of the Vikings focuses on Cousins. Falcons writers checking in on former players said Cousins fell flat against the 49ers.
  • Atlanta writers call a win a major upset. Falcons coverage says Atlanta needs consistent, complementary football to win in Minnesota.

What the national media is saying

  • Falcons: National coverage frames the game around Atlanta's 2024 double move at quarterback, signing Cousins and then drafting Penix, and some analysts call the draft pick the bigger mistake.
  • Falcons: National writers expect trouble for a Falcons line that has struggled to protect all season against Minnesota's pressure.
  • Vikings: National critics say the 49ers blowout exposed Kevin O'Connell's struggles in big games, a problem they say has been building for years.
  • Vikings: National coverage still calls the defense Minnesota's greatest strength, even after San Francisco showed how to beat it.

Who's out

  • Did not practice: Falcons guard Matthew Bergeron, cornerback Billy Bowman Jr., receivers Zachariah Branch and Jahan Dotson, safety DeMarcco Hellams, and linebacker Za'Darius Smith.
  • Limited: Falcons defensive end James Pearce Jr.
  • Did not practice: Vikings receiver Jordan Addison, tackle Christian Darrisaw, defensive tackle Jalen Redmond, cornerback Isaiah Rodgers, and safety Harrison Smith.
  • Limited: Vikings receivers Tai Felton and Myles Price and quarterback Kyler Murray.
