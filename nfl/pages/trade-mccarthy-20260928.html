Giants Make Early-Season Move: JJ McCarthy from Vikings

New York acquired the second-year quarterback from Minnesota in one of the first significant trades of the 2026 season, gambling on a former top-10 pick who lost his starting job.

THE QUICK TAKE
  • McCarthy, 23, was the No. 10 overall pick in the 2024 NFL Draft and started 11 games as a rookie before losing his job in the 2026 preseason.
  • Minnesota received a fifth-round pick — a price most analysts described as embarrassingly low for a former top-10 pick.
  • New York's starter had been struggling, and the front office decided McCarthy's upside at 23 warranted a low-cost acquisition.
  • The D grade for Minnesota reflects that the Vikings massively undersold an asset with real remaining value. Even a struggling young quarterback commands more than a fifth-round pick.
  • McCarthy's 2025 stat line (2,847 yards, 18 TD, 12 INT) was not elite, but his age and draft pedigree suggested the market should have been much higher.
What New York Media Is Saying About the New York Giants

New York coverage ranged from cautiously optimistic to genuinely excited. McCarthy is 23 and a former top-10 pick — the Giants paid a fifth-round pick for a player with that profile, which feels like smart opportunism.

Local analysts acknowledged the risk: McCarthy's first season was inconsistent, and there is no guarantee he develops. But the cost is low enough that this is an easy bet to make.

What Minnesota Media Is Saying About the Minnesota Vikings

Minnesota coverage was harsh. A fifth-round pick for the No. 10 overall pick from two years ago is a terrible return in any market. The Vikings clearly needed to move on from McCarthy, but the execution drew significant criticism.

Local analysts and national media both noted that the Vikings should have been able to command at least a second or third-round pick. The fact that they settled for a fifth suggests either urgency or poor negotiating — neither is flattering.

WHAT EVERYONE ELSE IS SAYING

Nationally, this was held up as an example of a team selling a draft pick at the absolute wrong price. 'Minnesota left multiple rounds on the table,' wrote The Athletic. New York was widely praised for identifying value and executing quickly. The Giants got the steal of the early-season trade market.

WIN-WIN OR FLEECED?

New York wins easily. The Giants acquired a 23-year-old former top-10 pick for a fifth-round pick — that's exceptional value regardless of how McCarthy develops. Minnesota's D grade is unusually harsh, but it reflects genuine front-office miscalculation in a transaction that should never have closed at that price.
