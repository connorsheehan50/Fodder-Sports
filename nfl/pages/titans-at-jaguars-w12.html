The summary

  • The #6-ranked Jaguars (7-3) host the #30-ranked Titans (1-9) two weeks after winning 40-23 in Nashville. Jacksonville leads the AFC South but lost 23-18 to the Giants last week, ending a three-game winning streak.
  • Tennessee has allowed 134 points over its last three games, including a 51-30 loss at Dallas, and head coach Robert Saleh called his defense's play "embarrassing football."
  • The Jaguars have not lost a regular-season home game since Week 6 of last season. Their run game has been held to a season-low 77 yards twice in recent weeks, with running back Bhayshul Tuten playing through an ankle and foot problem.
  • Titans quarterback Cam Ward has played his best football over the last month, and rookie receiver Carnell Tate, the fourth overall pick, did not play in the first meeting.

The word on the Titans

  • Tennessee media is embarrassed. Local coverage calls the Dallas loss one of the worst defensive performances in franchise history and says jobs are on the line.
  • Local writers are already looking ahead. Titans coverage asks which defensive starters need replacing in 2027 and finds a silver lining in draft position.
  • Jacksonville coverage sees a game to get right. Jaguars writers say the team really needs a response, and it has been strikingly good at home and after losses under head coach Liam Coen.
  • Jacksonville respects Ward. "He's got a lot of confidence right now," Coen said of Ward. "I think you're seeing the guy just play better and better."

The word on the Jaguars

  • Jacksonville media got a reality check. Local coverage called the Giants loss weird and maddening, and some say it proves the Jaguars have a long way to go to be real contenders.
  • Local writers want the run game back. Jaguars coverage says the team needs to return to its identity as a physical running team. "You're glad to be home, and you should be," Lawrence said.
  • Tennessee's coverage of the Jaguars remembers the last meeting. Titans coverage lists the 40-23 loss to Jacksonville as part of a stretch of 40-point games allowed.
  • Tennessee is focused on itself. Titans coverage has said little about Jacksonville this week, with the focus on Saleh's scheme and the defense.

What the national media is saying

  • Titans: National coverage is harsh on the Titans, calling the Dallas game their most humiliating loss yet, though Ward defended Saleh afterward.
  • Titans: National writers expect another long afternoon in the rematch, though defensive lineman Jacob Martin, with a career-high 7.5 sacks, is a bright spot.
  • Jaguars: National coverage called the Giants game a nightmare day for the Jaguars, with Lawrence's two interceptions and two dropped snaps.
  • Jaguars: National writers say Jacksonville's hopes for the top seed are fading, but another playoff opportunity awaits.
